The best repair is the one that makes you unnecessary.

03.01 — Educator

While developing the STEM Public Welfare Course Kit for Adolescent Students Based on RC Cars, I kept thinking about how to bring it to more schools so that more students could experience RC-based STEM education. The first step is developing the teaching equipment, the textbooks, the lesson plans and the feedback forms.

03.02 — Educator

With everything ready, I led club members to four primary and secondary schools across Hubei Province to teach STEM courses. We donated full sets of teaching equipment to two of them and set up WeChat groups to train their follow-up teachers, so the courses could sustain themselves. The courses were embraced by students and teachers alike, which drove me to keep improving, and to publish a public appeal inviting more people to help close the “Experience Gap” of technology education.

03.03 — Educator

This summer, I brought all of my RC teaching equipment back to the Wuhan Science and Technology Museum, a place I visited countless times as a child, to run open STEM classes. In that mobile classroom, I once again felt how deeply children and parents love RC-based STEM courses.

03.04 — Educator

At every school event I keep promoting RC education, and I have organized several charity fairs — donating all proceeds to the ISA Foundation.

03.05 — Educator

The STEM Education Equity Initiative has been shortlisted for the Fosun Foundation 2025–2026 Infinite Visionaries Award, and that will keep me teaching many more public welfare STEM courses.
