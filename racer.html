Racer

Racing taught me that speed depends on understanding, not reflexes.

01.01 — Racer

I first encountered RC racing in our school's RC club when I was in fourth grade. The feeling of driving my own car flat-out around the track was simply wonderful. For eight years since, I have trained professionally at Guangzhou Feiteng Circuit and Yongkang Feishen Circuit: on-road and off-road, electric and nitro, I love them all.

01.02 — Racer

The moment I started Grade 10, I couldn’t wait to found the ISA Wuhan RC Racing Club. Our Wednesday-afternoon sessions remain unforgettable: we research, train, race and take part in school events together, gaining friendship, joy and success.

01.03 — Racer

Exchange matters. I led the club to study with six racing teams at Wuhan University of Technology, and joined students from Shanghai to co-found the High School Society of Race Car Technologies (HSRT), a national league for high schoolers. I authored its technical regulations and have fielded a team in every season, learning from one another along the way.

01.04 — Racer

Eight years of racing have brought championships at national, provincial, municipal and district levels, championship prize money, and the National Level-2 Athlete Certificate, plus plenty of team medals as a captain.

01.05 — Racer

Most important of all, I learned to tune and repair cars, the foundation for everything I later researched. In racing, the result is truly decided in the pit.
